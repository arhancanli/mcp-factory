# UK calendar snapshot

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Source: [GOV.UK bank holidays](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json).
Terms: [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
The JSON records its observation date and the hash of the original response.
Dates, names and substitution notes for 2026-2028 are retained for England and Wales, Scotland, and
Northern Ireland. The runtime uses this snapshot without network requests.
