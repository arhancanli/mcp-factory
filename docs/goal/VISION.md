# Quantavane vision (owner-directed, 2026-10-21)

**One line.** The engine for verified discovery, in every field: models propose, machines and labs check, and only
what's proven is kept.

**The insight.** AI has made ideas cheap. Checking them is now the bottleneck. Quantavane builds the checkers, the
search that runs against them, and the models trained on everything the search learns. Every answer makes the engine
better at the next one. No branch is the whole story: every branch gets its own checker and its own way of working
(see [BRANCHES.md](BRANCHES.md)).

## Every field at full depth (owner, 2026-10-21)

No single field is the spotlight. The core sciences each get a full programme with six named tracks, their own method,
a hard goal and a stated checker: mathematics, computing, physics, chemistry and materials, biology and medicine,
engineering and space. Every other branch (climate and Earth, oceans, astronomy, food and farming, brains, diagnosis,
animal communication, lost languages, finance, security, logistics and industry, AI itself) gets its own goal and
its own kind of proof. Maths and computing start first because their checkers are instant.

## The landmark goals (hard on purpose)

Each is a target, counted only with evidence. None is lowered because an early run looks weak; trial runs on free
infrastructure never define the lab's scores (owner, 2026-10-21).

1. **1,000 verified discoveries** published with rerunnable checkers, across at least ten branches.
2. **A famous open problem settled** in mathematics, with a proof a computer checks.
3. **An algorithm the world runs on**: a Quantavane result inside software used on a billion devices.
4. **Physical things that beat the best**: a designed material, chip, enzyme or device built and measured above the
   previous record.
5. **A designed medicine in human trials** with partners and regulators.
6. **Two-way exchange with another species**, checked by playback experiments.
7. **A lost script read**, with readings that hold on newly found inscriptions.
8. **Thousands of models used across the world**: discovery models, thousands of specialists, industry flagships,
   general flagships at the frontier, and the models that power every sister startup, released open or through an
   API on Cirrun, each only after beating the best alternative on a test it has never seen.
9. **The benchmark AI labs report against**: the Open Problems Benchmark, with hidden answers and real entries.
10. **The open-source home of AI tools**: thousands of best-in-class MCP servers, clean-room open reimplementations,
    and millions of GitHub stars from real adoption.
11. **First place where we compete**: Kaggle, AIMO, ARC Prize and research challenges, entered as Quantavane, losses
    published.
12. **A group of companies built on it**: GIMBIX, Cirrun, TraceAxiom, CanliCapital and new spin-outs running on
    Quantavane models and Cirrun compute.

## New rules, not just new records (owner, 2026-10-21)

The engine doesn't only beat records. It also explores without a target: it finds patterns in mathematics and in
data, proposes the rule behind them, and keeps only rules that survive a proof or a prediction on data they were
never fitted to. Targets: new mathematics (conjectures found by the engine, then proved), new laws of physics
(equations found in data that predict later measurements), new ways to compute (algorithm families and models of
computation unlike human designs), new chemistry, new rules of life, and open exploration. Maths, physics and
computing get the deepest programmes on the site (eight tracks each) because they are where the biggest breakthroughs
can be checked soonest.

## Vision upgrades (2026-10-21, owner asked for the best reasoning on making the vision stronger)

**How Quantavane becomes the frontier lab.** Not by out-spending others on internet text, which every lab has. The
next jumps in AI come from training against results that can be checked (that is how today's reasoning models were
made, using maths and code). Quantavane owns the largest supply of checkable training signal in the world: checkers
for every field, plus real measurements from its labs and factories in every sector. Add the cheapest compute
anywhere (Cirrun, with our own chips and power), and distribution through every Quantavane company and every startup
it powers. Order: frontier in each science first (the best maths prover, the best materials model, the best chip
designer), then our own pretrained models, then frontier general models.

**Superhuman discovery that people can check.** As AI passes human experts, the hard question becomes "how do we
trust it?" Quantavane's answer is built in: every claim comes with its proof or its measurement. That makes
Quantavane the lab governments, scientists and companies trust most as AI gets more powerful.

**One north star for the whole group: verified progress per day,** the count of checked improvements (records,
discoveries, better products) produced every day across every company. It measures "leading humanity" directly.

**"Top of the top" as an operating rule.** Every company names the best competitor in its field and the yardstick
it is measured on, and is measured against it every quarter: lead it, or fix the approach.

**Talent no other lab can offer.** Top researchers can lead or found a Quantavane company in their own field, with
real equity; the most compute per researcher; open publication of proven results; Quantavane Fellows and prizes for
open problems; competitions as the hiring funnel; research hubs in the world's talent centres.

**Open where it spreads, closed where it compounds.** The engine, specialist models, benchmarks and the attempt
archive are open, for reach, stars and talent. Flagship weights and the real-world data stay inside.

**A library of everything proven.** Every verified theorem, material property, measurement and algorithm goes into one
open, machine-checkable library that every model trains on: a public good, and the foundation for every next search.

**Partnerships with nations.** Countries building their own AI capacity, starting with the Gulf where the UAE is
building one of the world's largest AI campuses, can run a national discovery engine with Quantavane.

**Safety and trust as a strength.** A frontier-grade safety programme (model evaluations, red-teaming, staged
release rules) attracts the best researchers and earns regulators' trust in every sector the group enters.

## How the lab grows

The same loop runs everywhere, but the checker decides how fast a branch can move:

- **Instant checkers** (maths, algorithms, chips in simulation, finance on untouched data, AI benchmarks): start now,
  run around the clock, most results per dollar.
- **Simulation checkers** (physics, materials, energy, space, climate models): start with open simulators, then
  confirm with partners.
- **Lab and field checkers** (biology, medicine, agriculture, ageing, animal communication, robotics): start with
  open benchmarks and public data, then partner labs and field teams, then Quantavane's own automated labs.
- **Time checkers** (forecasts, lost languages): a claim is scored on data that didn't exist when it was made.

Safety runs through every branch: biology designs are screened before they're made and nothing is made more harmful;
security work is defensive with responsible disclosure; finance claims are tested only on data the search never saw.
