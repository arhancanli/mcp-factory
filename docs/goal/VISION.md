# Quantavane vision (owner-directed, 2026-10-21)

**One line.** The engine for verified discovery: models propose, machines and labs check, only what's proven is kept,
from new mathematics to new medicines.

**The insight.** AI has made ideas cheap. Checking them is now the bottleneck. Quantavane builds the checkers, the
search that runs against them, and the models trained on everything the search learns. Every answer makes the engine
better at the next one.

**Biology is the biggest prize** (owner, 2026-10-21: tilt toward biology-related industries). Models can design
proteins and DNA in seconds, but testing takes weeks. Quantavane connects the engine to automated labs so design,
build, measure and learn run as one loop.
- Tracks: enzymes (plastic breakdown, oil-free chemicals), medicines (antibodies and drug candidates for neglected
  diseases), antibiotics against resistant bacteria, gene switches (cell-specific DNA), crops (drought and heat,
  cleaner fertiliser), virtual cells (predict responses before the experiment), later ageing.
- Checker ladder: open benchmarks with hidden answers, then partner labs, then Quantavane's own automated lab.
  Computational predictions are proposals, never results.
- Industries served: drug discovery, agriculture and food, industrial biology and chemicals, diagnostics.
- Safety: every design screened before synthesis; only labs that follow biosafety rules; no work that makes a
  pathogen or toxin more dangerous.

**Road (targets).** 2027 biology benchmarks; 2028 first protein designs tested with partner labs; 2029 lab scientists
hired; 2030 own automated lab; 2031 medicines track; 2032 own labs as part of the institute; 2034 drug candidates in
partner trials.
