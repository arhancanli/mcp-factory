# Quantavane ten-year plan, month by month (Nov 2026 – Oct 2036)

Rewritten 2026-10-22 at the owner's request ("what must we do to reach all of our ultimate goals, month by month for
10 years"). It replaces the 2026-10-21 version: the money path now follows the owner's bootstrap decision (no angel,
seed or Series A rounds), and no target from that version moved later. Years 1-2 are the working plan; from year 3
each month is a target date, re-planned every quarter from real results and never lowered. Goals: VISION.md,
QUANTAVANE.md, GROUP.md, BRANCHES.md.

## What it takes

Five things happen every month for ten years; the calendar below is these five on dates:
1. **Checked results, more every month.** North star: verified progress per day.
2. **Models that get better every day.** Every result and every customer's data trains the next version.
3. **Outside money.** Products, projects and partnerships that bring in revenue, so no small rounds are ever needed.
4. **A new Quantavane company whenever a field has a checked result and a paying customer.**
5. **The best people.** Any great researcher can lead or found a Quantavane company, with equity.

  Computing starts evolving sorting, hashing and compression code against the best public versions.

  models). Open Problems Benchmark v0 with hidden answers, and a live results page on quantavane.com.

## How it's paid for (owner decision 2026-10-21: no small equity rounds)

- **Now to 2027:** $0 local models and free tiers, then stacked credits once the company exists, plus prize money.
  Grants that take no equity (Emergent Ventures, Thiel Fellowship) are optional, the owner's call.
- **2027-2029:** outside revenue: optimisation projects, the model API, design partnerships paid upfront plus
  milestones, government contracts. Cirrun takes over compute at written prices.
- **2029-2032:** revenue from the Quantavane companies and the platforms they power; pharma and industry
  partnerships; medicine grants that take no equity; data centres and plants financed with debt against long
  contracts, as the big AI builders do.
- **2032-2036, frontier scale (tens of billions in total):** group revenue, compute partnerships with nations, debt
  for data centres and plants, and only if the owner chooses, one late, large raise or a listing, always with
  founder-controlled shares.

Rules that hold throughout: a claim counts only with its checker or measurement; the owner approves every public
word; biology designs screened; security work defensive; lab spending only from won funding.
