# Quantavane ten-year plan

Written 2026-10-21 from the owner's goals. Years are targets; each stage starts when the one before has real,
verified results. Funding amounts are spent only in the owner's stated amounts.

**2027: prove the engine.** Discovery engine built and open-sourced. First published maths records beaten, each with a
checker. Site live, first competitions, first Kaggle wins as Quantavane. First MCP servers released. Grants
(Emergent Ventures, AI for Math Fund round) and cloud credits. Company formed, with parents signing. First tuned open
models deployed in GIMBIX and Cirrun. Target: 1,000 to 10,000 GitHub stars.

**2028: become known.** Algorithms and physics tracks producing results; first arXiv papers and workshop
acceptances. Open Problems Benchmark launched and adopted by some AI labs. Paid optimisation projects for outside
companies. First angel or pre-seed round. Dozens of specialised models. Young researchers programme running. First
part-time paid researchers. Target: 100 MCP servers, 10,000+ stars.

**2029: build the team.** Seed round. First full-time researchers. Owner turns 18 and can sign for the company.
Hundreds of specialised models; first flagship industry models with paying customers. All sibling startups running
on Quantavane models where they measurably win. Benchmark reported by major labs. Target: 500 MCP servers, 100,000
stars.

**2030: own models.** First Quantavane model trained from scratch at mid-size, competitive on public benchmarks.
Series A. Biotech branch opens computationally, with the first lab partners. Large Cirrun-backed compute.

**2031: many branches.** Biotech results in partner labs; materials and energy branches open. Animal communication
project launched with field partners. Discovery API used by universities and companies. First famous open problem
settled, the breakthrough target.

**2032: an institute.** Research campus or hubs; hundreds of staff. Model family competitive with frontier labs in
reasoning and science. Moonshot portfolio running across five or more branches. Thousands of MCP servers.

**2033: industry engine.** Flagship industry models powering whole sectors. Medicine branch working with clinical
partners and regulators. Sibling startups fully vertically integrated on Quantavane models and Cirrun compute.

**2034: frontier.** Frontier-scale flagship models, funded by revenue and major rounds. Results in textbooks.
1,000,000 GitHub stars across the organisation.

**2035: the world's problems.** Governments, universities and companies send their hardest problems to the engine.
Projects in every branch of MOONSHOTS.md running.

**2036: the engine for the world's science.** Quantavane is the leading lab for verified discovery, with its own
frontier models, its own compute through Cirrun, and a group of companies built on top.
