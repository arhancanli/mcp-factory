## Every company integrated into Quantavane (and the sister companies when needed)

Every Quantavane company, science, industry or platform, is wired in the same seven ways from day one:
1. **Models:** a dedicated model branch inside Quantavane (its own team and model stack: the flagship for broad work,
   specialists for its own tasks).
2. **Training:** its data and its checkers feed the always-on training system below.
3. **Engine:** its hardest problems run on the discovery engine.
4. **Compute:** all training and serving on Cirrun, on its own account at written prices.
5. **Routing and cost:** GIMBIX sends every request to the best model for the job at the lowest cost.
6. **Trust:** TraceAxiom checks every AI agent action and code change that touches real systems, such as labs,
   factories and money.
7. **Capital and markets:** CanliCapital's research where markets and capital decisions are involved.

Inside each company, vertical integration climbs the ladder from partner labs to its own lab, pilot plant and
full-scale production (GROUP.md). The best possible approach for each company comes from its branch entry in
BRANCHES.md, and is itself improved by the engine over time.

## The always-on training system (every company's models improve every day)

One loop per company, running around the clock:
1. **Capture:** every attempt, lab measurement, factory reading, product interaction (with permission) and failure,
   as versioned data.
2. **Measure:** each company keeps a hidden test suite built from its checkers; every model is scored every day.
3. **Choose:** for each task, a router uses whichever model is winning right now, flagship or specialist, weighing
   quality, speed and cost.
4. **Improve:** training never stops:
   - reinforcement learning against the checkers, with the checker as the reward;
   - distilling flagship skill into small, fast specialists;
   - feeding specialists' verified wins back into flagship training;
   - the engine searching for better training recipes themselves.
5. **Promote:** a challenger replaces the current champion only after winning the hidden tests and a live shadow
   trial, with automatic rollback.
6. **Schedule:** training fills every idle hour on Cirrun, so improvement never pauses.

Guardrails: no regressions ship; contamination checks on every test set; safety evaluations; customers' data rights.

## Reaching points no one thought possible

**Quantavane improves itself.** The engine runs its search on its own code: training methods, kernels, compilers
and data selection. AlphaEvolve showed this works: Google reported it recovered about 0.7% of Google's worldwide
compute and cut Gemini's training time by about 1%.

**Quantavane builds the stack it runs on.** Computing makes the algorithms, Silicon the chips, Materials the
semiconductors and batteries, and Energy the power, all on Cirrun's data centres. Each company makes AI cheaper and
better for all the others: AI designing the chips, power and algorithms that train the next AI.

**Every company makes every other company smarter.** Physics models help materials, chemistry helps biology, and
proven results become building blocks for the next search.

**Targets that sound impossible today:** a new machine-checked theorem every day; a material designed, made and
measured in a single day; a medicine from idea to trial-ready in about a year; chips designed in weeks instead of
years; laws of physics found directly in data; every company's models measurably better every week.
