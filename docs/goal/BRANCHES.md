# Quantavane branches: a specialised approach for each (2026-10-21)

Branches differ in what counts as proof, how fast a check runs, what data exists and who has to be involved. Each
entry says: the landmark goal, how candidates are proposed, the checker, data and models, partners or equipment, the
first step, and the industries it serves. A branch opens when its first step is funded; a claim counts only when its
checker passes.

## Instant-checker branches (start first)

**Mathematics.** Landmark: settle a famous open problem; machine-check whole areas of maths in Lean.
Propose: evolved construction programs, and model-written Lean proofs. Checker: exact code (rational arithmetic) and
Lean. Data: every attempt, plus Mathlib. Partners: the mathematicians holding current records, credited before any
announcement. First step: less-crowded problems with a published best-known value; then Erdős problems with Lean
statements. Serves: research, cryptography, chip verification.

**Algorithms and computing.** Landmark: a Quantavane algorithm upstream in software that runs on a billion devices.
Propose: evolved kernels, schedulers and compressors. Checker: correctness tests plus benchmarks on fixed hardware
against the best public implementation. Data: open benchmark suites. Partners: maintainers of the libraries we
improve. First step: sorting, hashing and compression kernels; results deployed in Cirrun first. Serves: cloud,
databases, compilers, every software company.

**AI itself.** Landmark: the Open Problems Benchmark becomes what AI labs report; Quantavane models first on named
leaderboards. Propose: training recipes and model variants. Checker: hidden test sets, contamination checks, versioned
seasons. Data: the lab's attempt archive. First step: benchmark v0 from problems the engine works on; competitions
with our baselines. Serves: every AI lab and company choosing models.

**Chips (design).** Landmark: an AI-designed circuit fabricated and measured beating the human design. Propose:
circuit and layout programs. Checker: logic simulation and formal equivalence first; timing, power and area with open
tools; fabrication through shuttle runs later. Partners: open-silicon programmes, then fabs. First step: open EDA
benchmarks. Serves: semiconductors; possible spin-out.

**Finance (with CanliCapital).** Landmark: research the industry trusts because luck is ruled out. Checker: untouched
out-of-sample data only; backtests alone never count. Serves: funds, banks. Lives inside CanliCapital and ALPHAC.

**Security and cryptography (with the post-quantum startup).** Landmark: implementations proven correct and attacks
found before criminals find them. Checker: proofs about implementations, test vectors, reproducible exploits in
sandboxes; disclosure before publication. Defensive only. Serves: banks, governments, cloud.

**Software and tools.** Landmark: thousands of best-in-class MCP servers and clean-room open reimplementations used
by millions. Checker: head-to-head tests against the best alternative before release; clean-room legal record per
project. Serves: every developer; drives stars and adoption.

## Simulation-checker branches

**Physics.** Landmark: new exact solutions and better bounds in fundamental physics. Propose: symbolic derivations
and numerical constructions. Checker: symbolic verification plus independent numerical check. Partners: theorists.

**Engineering and design.** Landmark: antennas, optics and structures nobody has drawn, built and measured above the
record. Checker: open electromagnetic and structural simulators, then prototypes from partners or makerspaces.
Serves: telecoms, aerospace, consumer devices.

**Materials and chemistry.** Landmark: a new battery, catalyst or superconductor material made and measured.
Propose: structures and recipes. Checker: physics simulation (DFT and learned potentials) as a filter, then
synthesis and measurement by partner labs and later robots. Serves: batteries, solar, chemicals.

**Energy and fusion.** Landmark: plasma control and reactor designs run on a real device. Checker: plasma simulators,
then partner experiments. Serves: fusion companies, grids.

**Space.** Landmark: mission and orbit designs flown by partners. Checker: physics simulation to flight-grade
tolerance. Serves: launch and satellite companies.

**Climate and Earth.** Landmark: forecasts that beat the best centres, and carbon-capture materials that work.
Checker: forecasts scored only on observations that came after the forecast; materials as above. Serves: insurers,
agriculture, governments.

## Lab and field branches (computational first, then partners, then our own labs)

**Biology.** Landmark: designed proteins and DNA that work first time; an automated lab where models design and
robots build and measure around the clock. Checker ladder: open benchmarks with hidden answers, then partner labs,
then our own automated lab. Safety: every design screened before synthesis; nothing made more harmful. Serves: drug
discovery, industrial biology, food.

**Medicine.** Landmark: a designed medicine in human trials. Checker: assays, then animal studies, then clinical
trials with partners and regulators. Focus: diseases that get too little attention, antibiotics for resistant
bacteria. Serves: pharma, health systems.

**Agriculture and food.** Landmark: crops that hold yield in drought and heat; fertiliser that pollutes less.
Checker: greenhouse, then field trials across seasons. Serves: farming, food companies.

**Ageing.** Landmark: interventions that slow ageing in cells, then extend healthy life in model organisms. Checker:
cell assays, then lifespan studies with partners.

**Neuroscience.** Landmark: models that predict recorded brain activity better than any other. Checker: held-out
neural recordings from public datasets and partners.

**Animal communication.** Landmark: two-way exchange with another species. Propose: models of whale, bird, elephant
and primate signals. Checker: playback experiments with field researchers, under their welfare rules.

**Lost languages.** Landmark: read Linear A or the Indus script. Checker: readings must hold on inscriptions not used
to make them, and on newly found ones.

**Robotics.** Landmark: robots that learn a new physical skill in minutes. Checker: simulation, then real-robot
trials. Serves: manufacturing, logistics.

**Oceans.** Landmark: maps of ocean life from sound and sensors that predict new surveys. Checker: later surveys.

## Industries

Every branch feeds flagship industry models, each trained on the industry's own data and checked by its own tests:
pharma and biotech, agriculture and food, materials and chemicals, energy, semiconductors, telecoms, aerospace,
finance, security, cloud and software. A model replaces what an industry uses only when it measurably wins.
