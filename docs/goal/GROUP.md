# Quantavane: the models that power everything, and a company for every science and sector

Owner, 2026-10-21: "I'm planning on leading humanity here." Quantavane becomes everything we dream it will be: its
models power every company, and every science and every sector gets its own Quantavane company, each the best in
the world at what it does. This replaces the earlier draft of this file (which mapped smaller side projects and
asked basic structural questions; the owner set those aside).

## The core: Quantavane models

Frontier flagship models and thousands of specialists, trained on everything the engine and every Quantavane company
learns. They power every Quantavane company, the sister companies (GIMBIX, Cirrun, TraceAxiom, CanliCapital) and the
rest of the world through open models and the API.

## Science companies: every layer is its own company

Each owns its field's discoveries and sells the best tools in that field, with its own leader, team and investors.
- **Quantavane Mathematics:** new mathematics and proofs, plus the world's best optimisation and proof tools (the
  market Gurobi and Wolfram serve) and formal verification for chips, cryptography and aerospace.
- **Quantavane Physics:** new physics, plus AI simulation that replaces slow solvers (the market Ansys serves;
  Synopsys bought Ansys in 2025), fusion control and quantum designs.
- **Quantavane Chemistry:** molecule and reaction design and simulation (the market Schrödinger serves).
- **Quantavane Computing:** algorithms, compilers, databases and quantum algorithms, licensed everywhere.
- **Quantavane Biology, Earth and Climate, Astronomy, Neuroscience** and every other science, each its own company.

## Industry companies: vertically integrated

Materials, Bio and Medicine, Silicon, Energy, Robotics, Space, Food, Health and every further sector. Each owns its
whole loop, from models to its own labs, plants and factories to the products, climbing one rung at a time: partner
labs, own automated lab, own pilot plant, own full-scale production.

## Platform companies: Quantavane models in front of millions

- **A marketing and CRM platform at HubSpot scale,** where the AI does the marketing work, not just records it.
- **Generative platforms at Higgsfield scale:** video, images, audio and 3D, all on Quantavane models.
- More platforms wherever Quantavane models can be the best product in a category.

## Why this structure wins

- **Top of the top in each field:** every company has one job and its own leaders, so each can be the best in the
  world at it.
- **One brain:** every company runs on Quantavane models and sends its data back, so the models improve with every
  company added.
- **Cirrun grows with all of it:** every Quantavane company is its own Cirrun customer with its own budget, so Cirrun
  earns from dozens of independent accounts instead of one. Generative platforms are among the heaviest compute users
  there are. Each company pays Cirrun's normal written prices, Cirrun reports group revenue on its own line, and as
  each company raises outside money its spending counts as genuinely diversified revenue.
- **Each company raises its own capital** while Quantavane keeps control; founder-controlled shares at the top.

## Rules that hold everywhere

Fair written prices between companies; data flows back to Quantavane; a model replaces what a company uses only on a
measured win; public wording says "lead"; biology designs screened and security work defensive; stars only from
real adoption; clean-room open source; lab spending only from won funding; the owner approves every public word.
