The owner's aim: lead human progress by building the largest group of companies in the world, across every science,
every sector and every kind of business, all powered by one engine and one shared backbone. "We are trying to get
everything here, and that's the good part." This file keeps the whole picture in one place. Proposed top: the Canli
holding (owner's July 2026 plan), pending owner confirmation.

## 1. Quantavane, the brain

- **Research institutes, one per science, each with its own checker:** mathematics; physics; chemistry and
  materials; computing and algorithms; biology and medicine; engineering and space; the "new rules" exploration
  programme; and every other branch in [BRANCHES.md](BRANCHES.md). Each runs open problem lists, competitions,
  papers and prizes.
- **The discovery engine,** always on.
- **The model factory: thousands of models.** Discovery models; specialists; industry flagships; general frontier
  flagships; and a dedicated model branch for every group company (GIMBIX, Cirrun, TraceAxiom, CanliCapital,
  Curivela, the marketing arm, the games studio and every new startup). A model replaces what a company uses only on a
  measured win.
- **The talent pipeline:** the young researchers programme; public competitions (whoever beats our baselines gets
  noticed and offered work); Kaggle, AIMO and ARC Prize teams; paid fellowships; university partnerships; hiring top
  researchers; and learners coming up through the group's education companies.
- **The open layer:** the open-source engine, MCP servers for every niche, benchmarks and the attempt archive. This
  is the group's reach and its GitHub stars (legitimate adoption only).
- **The data:** every company's lab, factory, field and product data flows back here. No one else sees real
  measurements from that many industries.

## 2. Quantavane industry companies (vertically integrated)

One company per sector, each owning its whole loop: models from the engine, its own labs, plants and factories, and
the products. Majority-owned by Quantavane, each with an operator CEO from its industry and its own investors.
- **Ladder, one rung at a time:** lab track; company with partner labs and contract makers; own automated lab; own
  pilot plant; own full-scale factories, fabs or clinics and sales.
- **Order:** Materials (default first), Bio, Silicon; then Energy, Health and medicine, Robotics, Food; then Space,
  Climate, Oceans; then a new sector every year.
- **Rule to form one:** a verified result, a clear buyer, and funding for its first rung.

## 3. The backbone: sister companies every company plugs into on day one

Cirrun (compute; Quantavane's own compute moves here once credits run out, paid at written prices), GIMBIX (AI cost
and speed), TraceAxiom (proof that AI agents and code do what they claim), CanliCapital (financial research, later
capital), the post-quantum security startup (security), and the marketing company (distribution). Every new company
starts with compute, models, cost control, verification, research, security and distribution that no outside startup
gets.

## 4. The studio: many more startups across the branches

Built on the backbone. Ideas come from three places: Quantavane results, needs spotted across the backbone companies'
customer networks, and outside founders through a group ventures fund that backs startups built on our stack.

## 6. Suggested next additions

A ventures fund for outside founders on our stack; a foundation (open-science grants, access to education); a media
studio telling the discovery story; consumer devices designed by the engine; later health clinics and insurance,
payments, logistics, and telecoms through Space.

## The four flywheels

1. **Discovery and data:** discoveries become products, products produce data, data improves the models, and the
   next sector starts faster.
2. **Talent:** education audiences, then competitions and fellowships, then researchers, then discoveries and
   companies, which fund more education.
3. **Distribution:** marketing, social and education audiences become the first users of every new company.
4. **Capital:** profits from earlier companies and CanliCapital fund the next ones; each company also raises its own.

## Rules that hold everywhere

Fair written prices between group companies; data flows back to Quantavane; each company raises its own capital
while the group keeps control; founder-controlled shares at the top; all IP for the engine and models owned by
Quantavane; public wording says "lead", not "take over"; safety (biology designs screened, security defensive with
responsible disclosure); stars only from real adoption; clean-room open source; lab spending only from won funding;
the owner approves every public word.

## Order of building (targets)

- **Now to 2027:** the Quantavane engine and first records; the sister companies and Curivela grow; the marketing
  engines serve the group.
- **2028 to 2029:** formal talent pipeline; the first industry company; the studio's first startups on the backbone;
  the education enterprise; the marketing company takes outside clients.
- **2030 to 2032:** Bio and Silicon; the ventures fund; a social platform; the foundation.
- **2033 onward:** a new industry company every year; consumer devices; new sectors continuously.
